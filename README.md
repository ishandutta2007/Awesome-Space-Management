# Awesome-Space-Management

## Top Space Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Desk Booking, Room Reservation, Workplace Experience, Floor Plans, Occupancy Analytics & Hybrid Office Management*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Space Management**. These tools help organizations manage desks, meeting rooms, floor plans, occupancy, and hybrid workplace experiences so employees can find and book the right space while facilities teams optimize utilization.



**Examples** include OfficeSpace, Condeco (Eptura Engage), Robin, Eden, Envoy Desks / Workplace, Skedda, SpaceIQ, Eptura, FM:Systems, and Archibus (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise IWMS/space-management suites are mostly commercial. However, several solid open-source desk and room booking systems exist for hybrid workplaces. **Seatsurfing**, **Workplacify**, **WARP**, **OpenDesk**, **Roomy**, and related projects provide practical self-hosted alternatives. This section lists every major active project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[OfficeSpace Software](https://www.officespacesoftware.com/)**  

  End-to-end space management platform focused on space planning, move management, booking, and facilities workflows.



- **[Condeco / Eptura Engage](https://eptura.com/)**  

  Enterprise meeting-room and desk booking solution with deep Microsoft 365 integration and workplace analytics (part of the broader Eptura portfolio).



- **[Robin](https://robinpowered.com/)**  

  Modern workplace platform emphasizing interactive maps, desk and room booking, AI-assisted scheduling, and occupancy analytics.



- **[Eden](https://www.edenworkplace.com/)**  

  Workplace experience and operations platform covering desk booking, visitor management, and office services.



- **[Envoy Workplace / Desks](https://envoy.com/)**  

  Desk and room booking combined with visitor management, occupancy insights, and workplace security features.



- **[Skedda](https://www.skedda.com/)**  

  Popular mid-market space booking platform known for interactive floor plans, flexible booking rules, and per-space pricing.



- **[SpaceIQ / Eptura](https://eptura.com/)**  

  Broader workplace and IWMS capabilities including space planning, asset management, and booking (SpaceIQ is part of Eptura).



- **[FM:Systems, Archibus](https://www.fmsystems.com/)**  

  Established IWMS and facilities management platforms that include space management, planning, and related workplace modules.



- **[Other workplace platforms](https://www.skedda.com/)**  

  Additional solutions for hybrid office booking, occupancy sensing, and workplace experience.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Seatsurfing](https://github.com/seatsurfing)**  

  Open-source desk and room booking system for flexible workspaces and hot-desking. Features floor plans, real-time availability, mobile-friendly interface, and admin tools.



- **[Workplacify](https://github.com/igeligel/workplacify)**  

  Open-source desk reservation and scheduling platform designed for hybrid workplaces. Focuses on simple, employee-friendly booking as an alternative to commercial tools.



- **[WARP – Workspace Autonomous Reservation Program](https://github.com/sebo-b/warp)**  

  Open-source system for managing hybrid office space (assigned desks, hot desks, etc.) with booking, visibility of who is in the office, and mobile/PWA support.



- **[OpenDesk](https://github.com/kanwalnainsingh/OpenDesk)**  

  Open-source desk booking system aimed at helping organizations optimize office desk utilization and let employees reserve desks for in-office days.



- **[Roomy](https://github.com/pixlcrashr/roomy)**  

  Open-source self-service room and place reservation system with hierarchical spaces, permissions, QR check-in, and calendar views.



- **[Bookyp](https://github.com/geprog/bookyp)**  

  Open-source platform for managing and booking rooms and workspaces.



- **[RoomFlow and meeting-room systems](https://github.com/search?q=room+booking+OR+desk+booking+open+source)**  

  Full-stack open-source meeting room booking applications and related workplace reservation projects.



- **[Other workplace booking tools](https://github.com/search?q=hot+desk+OR+desk+reservation+OR+space+management)**  

  Community projects focused on hot-desking, floor-plan booking, and simple space calendars.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Calendar & resource booking engines**: General open-source calendar systems adapted for rooms and desks.

- **Floor-plan visualization libraries**: Tools for rendering interactive maps that can be paired with booking backends.

- **Occupancy & sensor integrations**: Open-source IoT and MQTT stacks used to feed real-time occupancy data.

- **Notification & calendar sync**: Components for email/Slack/Teams notifications and calendar integrations.

- **Analytics dashboards**: Grafana or custom dashboards built on booking and occupancy data.

- Lightweight CMMS or facilities tools that complement pure booking systems.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

For self-hosted desk and room booking, start with **Seatsurfing**, **Workplacify**, or **WARP**.  

These provide practical foundations for hybrid workplace needs without commercial licensing costs.  

Enterprise IWMS, advanced space planning, sensor-driven analytics, and multi-site portfolio management are still dominated by commercial platforms (OfficeSpace, Eptura/Condeco, Robin, Envoy, Archibus, etc.).  

Many organizations successfully combine open-source booking for core desk/room needs with commercial tools for planning, analytics, or visitor management.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Space management systems handle employee location and schedule data. Proper privacy controls, consent, data retention policies, and security practices are essential.

- Open-source booking tools offer transparency and cost control but require self-hosting, maintenance, and careful evaluation of features (floor plans, rules engines, analytics, integrations) against commercial alternatives.
